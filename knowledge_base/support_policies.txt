SUPPORT TICKET POLICIES

1. Ticket Status

An open ticket means that the support team is still working on
the customer's issue.

A closed ticket means that the issue has been resolved or the
support process has been completed.

A ticket that cannot be found may have an incorrect ticket ID
or may not exist in the system.

2. Delayed Tickets

If a ticket remains open for more than three days, the customer
should contact the support team and provide their ticket ID.

The support team should review the ticket and provide an update.

3. Ticket Information

Customers should provide their correct ticket ID when asking
about their ticket status.

Customers should never share their account passwords when
requesting support.

4. Escalation

If an issue remains unresolved after contacting the support team,
the customer may request that the issue be escalated for further
review.
